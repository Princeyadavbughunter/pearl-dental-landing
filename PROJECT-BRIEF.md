## Strategic positioning

Pearl Dental's strength is **advanced implant technology + ethical practice + patient comfort**. The page leads on that combination, not on price. ₹20,000 appears as an entry point and a credibility anchor, never as the headline argument — competing purely on price would undercut the ethical-practice positioning that is the clinic's real moat.

### Campaign angles (strongest first)

1. Complete implant care under one roof
2. Implant treatment with advanced digital technology (OPG + intraoral scan + laser)
3. 18 years of experienced implant dentistry
4. Conscious sedation for a comfortable dental experience
5. Advanced dental implants at ₹20,000
